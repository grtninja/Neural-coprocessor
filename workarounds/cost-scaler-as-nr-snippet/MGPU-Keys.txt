; mgpu.ini keys used for the measurements in Cost-Scaler-Mount-Guide.md.

; game has to be closed when they are changed.

; runs on the card that drives the display and its result goes straight out.

; frame instead of the composed visual being withdrawn. Omit it on an unpatched
; build.
;PanelOverPicture=1
